# E5 Omission Audit — Case Studies

## Largest coverage asymmetry: contactmusic.com

Covers Entertainment at 56.2% but Business at only 0.1% — a gap of 56.1%. This outlet has a strong topical focus, systematically neglecting stories outside its editorial niche.

## Widest outlet coverage variance: Entertainment

Top 5 outlets cover Entertainment at 56%-41%: contactmusic.com, thecelebritycafe.com, dailymail.co.uk, huffingtonpost.com, entertainmentwise.com. Bottom 5 drop to 3%-1%: bloomberg.com, reuters.com, techtimes.com, marketwatch.com, nasdaq.com. Coverage variance of 55.4% signals systematic omission by smaller outlets.
