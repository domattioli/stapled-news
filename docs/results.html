Results

Six Experiments: Recovery Under Synthetic Truth, Syndication Inversion, Majority Capture, and In-the-Wild Null

Online EM recovers planted parameters at ρ = 0.88. Wire duplication inverts recovery to ρ = −0.87 (fully repaired by dedup voting). Unreliable majorities are unrepair able (Reuters ranks last on ISOT). FakeNewsNet shows in-the-wild inversion (ρ = −0.16). Consensus-agreement is null against factuality (ρ = −0.10, CI [−0.24, 0.05]).

E1 Recovery

Online EM Recovers Planted Outlet Parameters at ρ = 0.88, Matching Batch Methods

Across 30 synthetic corpora with known outlet reliability parameters, online EM recovers planted truth at mean Spearman ρ ≈ 0.88 — on par with batch Dawid-Skene (0.90), ahead of weighted majority vote (0.87), and substantially better than plain majority vote (0.71). The streaming approximation incurs negligible loss. This isolates the estimator machinery as sound; failures in real data are due to unmet assumptions (unreliable majorities or syndication effects), not broken inference.

E1 recovery: Spearman ρ comparing planted vs. recovered outlet consensus-agreement across 30 seeds. Online EM dedup-on ρ = 0.88; batch Dawid-Skene 0.90; weighted majority 0.87; majority vote 0.71. Streaming introduces negligible loss. The dashed line marks the 0.8 usability gate.
E2 Syndication

Syndication Duplication Inverts Recovery to ρ = −0.87; Dedup Voting Fully Repairs It

When one unreliable wire story is duplicated at multiplicity m ∈ {1, 2, 5, 10, 20} and re-attributed as originating from different outlets, un-deduplicated recovery catastrophically degrades: ρ = 0.82 (m=1) → 0.73 (m=2) → 0.31 (m=10) → −0.87 (m=20). At m=20, the model inverts completely: it ranks the fabricated sources above the truth by construction, because the sheer repetition convinces EM that repetition signals consensus truth.

With fractional dedup voting enabled (each cluster counts as one vote regardless of size), recovery remains flat across the entire sweep: ρ = 0.78–0.84. This isolates syndication as one fully-repairable mechanism of majority-capture. The data alone contains enough signal to fix it.

E3 ISOT

ISOT Majority-Capture: Reuters Ranks 7/7 Despite Being the Only Truthful Source

ISOT: 42,681 articles. Reuters publishes ~6,000; six synthetic sources publish ~37,000. Reuters is structurally outnumbered 6:1 and ranks last by estimated consensus-agreement (AUC = 0.0). The model adopts the fabricated majority as consensus truth and treats Reuters as an outlier. Dedup voting deflates fake estimates (mean 0.83 → 0.67) but cannot reorder ranks — there is no signal in the data to do so. This is the failure mode that cannot be fixed by data alone. It requires an external anchor (held-out labels, human judgment) or a fundamental rethink of what consensus measures.

ISOT: 7 outlets ranked by estimated consensus-agreement. Reuters (blue, real, truthful) ranks 7/7. Six synthetic sources (red, fabricated) rank 1–6. The model cannot distinguish truth from synchronized falsehood when the majority lies.
E3b FakeNewsNet

In-the-Wild Inversion: Consensus-Agreement Rises With Fabrication Share (ρ = −0.16, p = 0.003)

FakeNewsNet: 21,575 articles, 341 outlets after alignment filtering. Held-out fact-checker labels from Gossip Cop and PolitiFact show: outlets carrying more fabricated articles rank higher on consensus-agreement (Spearman ρ = −0.16, p = 0.003, AUC = 0.09). Fabricated celebrity stories are heavily syndicated — low-quality sources co-cover the same tabloid material, so consensus-agreement measures co-coverage intensity, not truthfulness. This replicates E3's designed inversion in organic data and is statistically significant.

E4 Factuality Null

Consensus-Agreement Is Null Against MBFC Factuality: ρ = −0.10 (UCI, n=176 outlets)

FakeNewsNet (MBFC labels on 34 outlets): ρ = −0.11, CI [−0.46, 0.24]. UCI (422,703 headlines, 176 MBFC-rated outlets at the 10-article participation floor, 7,202 multi-outlet events): fact ρ = −0.10, CI [−0.24, 0.05]; ideological-lean ρ = 0.02, CI [−0.12, 0.17]. The null is precise and replicates. Two representative outlets are indistinguishable by consensus-agreement: breitbart.com (low MBFC factuality, ≥99.6% reliability) vs. abc.net.au (high MBFC, ≥99.6% reliability) — both cluster high, one factually bankrupt by external standards. Roughly 3% of UCI headlines carry explicit negation; negation markers carry no factuality signal. E3b (syndication-driven inversion) and E4 (external factuality null) together show consensus-agreement is unidentifiable from coverage patterns alone: it measures co-coverage intensity and popularity clustering, not truth.

Interactive scatter: x-axis = MBFC factual rating (low = red, mixed = yellow, high = blue), y-axis = estimated consensus-agreement. Marker size ∝ log(articles); 176 MBFC-rated outlets. Spearman ρ = −0.10, CI [−0.24, 0.05] — no correlation. See FakeNewsNet n=34 version.

What This Adds Up To

The estimator is sound. On synthetic data, online EM recovers planted parameters at ρ = 0.88 — matching batch Dawid-Skene and beating majority voting. The machinery works when its assumptions hold.

Consensus is not truth. Fitted consensus-agreement measures co-coverage intensity by construction. Syndication duplication inverts recovery to ρ = −0.87 (fully repairable via dedup voting). Unreliable majorities invert recovery irreparably (Reuters ranks last on ISOT). The in-the-wild inversion on FakeNewsNet (ρ = −0.16) replicates this: fabricated stories cluster, so consensus masquerades as corroboration. Consensus-agreement null against factuality (ρ = −0.10, CI [−0.24, 0.05] on UCI) confirms the estimand mismatch.

Omission auditing is the honest use case. Consensus-agreement estimates which outlets systematically omit claims the broader field corroborates, and which propagate outlier stories. Label-free, not available from supervised outlet classifiers.

STAPLE-News Consensus Principle. Outlet consensus-agreement is a structural property of coverage, not a verdict on factuality. Estimates measure co-coverage intensity and popularity clustering. High consensus-agreement can reflect accuracy or synchronized misinformation. Use consensus estimates to audit omission and understand outlet ecosystem structure. Do not use to rank truthfulness.
