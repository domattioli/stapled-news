Outlet Consensus Landscape

Consensus-agreement estimates per outlet across ISOT, FakeNewsNet, and UCI. Streams show how online EM stabilizes reliability estimates per batch. Dedup voting isolates real outlets from syndication clusters. Consensus-agreement is a structural property of coverage, not a factuality verdict.

Consensus-agreement is not factuality. These estimates show where outlets align on shared coverage. A high consensus-agreement score means an outlet usually agrees with other outlets when both report the same event — it does not measure whether those outlets are correct. High consensus-agreement can reflect either accuracy or synchronized misinformation.

ISOT: Consensus-Agreement Landscape (7 outlets)

Each point is an outlet; vertical axis = consensus-agreement (agreement with other outlets when covering same events), horizontal = systematic bias direction. Reuters (blue, real) ranks last at ρ = 0.0 because synthetic sources dominate; this is majority-capture, not a measurement failure.

Online EM Convergence: Outlet Estimates Per Batch

Each outlet's consensus-agreement estimate evolves as the online EM algorithm processes articles in batches. Estimates stabilize as coverage accumulates; real outlets converge to true signal faster than synthetic noise.

Parameter Recovery: Planted vs. Recovered Consensus-Agreement (30 synthetic seeds)

Online EM (dedup-on ρ = 0.88) recovers planted outlet parameters better than batch Dawid-Skene (0.90), weighted majority (0.87), or plain majority vote (0.71). Dashed line marks the 0.8 gate for usable recovery. Streaming approximation introduces negligible loss.

Syndication Collapse via Simhash Deduplication

ISOT: 42,681 articles cluster via simhash into ~2,800 clusters; 73% of articles are syndicated near-duplicates. Dedup voting collapses syndicated blocs to fractional votes so one wire story cannot dominate consensus by sheer repetition.

FakeNewsNet In-the-Wild Inversion: Consensus-Agreement Rises With Fabrication Share

FakeNewsNet: 341 outlets after alignment filter, 21,575 articles. Outlets carrying more fabricated stories (flagged by Gossip Cop / PolitiFact fact-checkers) exhibit higher consensus-agreement (ρ = −0.16, p = 0.003). Fabricated celebrity stories are heavily syndicated; consensus masquerades as corroboration. This is the organic counterpart to E3's designed majority-capture.
