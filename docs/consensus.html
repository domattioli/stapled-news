How Far Each Outlet Sits From the Consensus Headline

A 30-day rolling corpus of German political frontpages. Every story counted is covered by five or more outlets. Distance is character n-gram cosine from each headline to its story's consensus centroid.

Corpus is German-language. Frontpage teasers carry no bylines, so results are reported at the outlet level — an author-level breakdown is not possible with this corpus. Distance measures headline divergence from cross-outlet consensus, not accuracy.
