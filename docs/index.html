Consensus Is Not Truth: When the Majority of Outlets Is Wrong, the Model Follows Them

Across 292,513 articles from 11,902 outlets, a streaming EM algorithm estimates which claims the press agrees on. It inverts predictably when fabricated stories dominate coverage, sharpening the evidence from three independent corpora (ISOT, FakeNewsNet, UCI).
